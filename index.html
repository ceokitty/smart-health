Security, privacy & ethics

Trust is the foundation

Compliance

  • HIPAA protects patient data (US)
  • GDPR Art. 9: health data is a special category (EU)
  • EU AI Act: medical AI is high-risk

Encryption

  • AES-256 for data at rest
  • TLS 1.3 for data in transit
  • Zero-trust access + audit logs

Ethics

  • Bias audits on diverse data
  • Explainable predictions
  • Human doctor in the loop
